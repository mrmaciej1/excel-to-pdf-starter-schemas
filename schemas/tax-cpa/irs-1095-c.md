## Known Implementation Pitfalls

1. **Leading-Zero Destruction in Excel:** Both `Plan_Start_Month` (`01`) and East Coast `ZIP` codes (`02110`, `07102`) lose their leading `0` if you double-click the CSV in Excel. Always import via **Data → From Text/CSV** with Text column types, or use a Text-formatted `.xlsx` workbook.
2. **All-12-Months vs. Monthly Columns:** Full-year employees with identical coverage all year use `f1_18[0]`, `f1_31[0]`, and `f1_44[0]`. Mid-year hires (e.g., waiting period `1H`/`2D` transitioning to `1E`/`2C`) must leave the All-12-Months cells blank and populate the 12 individual month columns (`f1_19[0]`..`f1_30[0]`, etc.).
3. **Electronic Filing Mandate:** Employers filing 10 or more combined information returns must e-file via the IRS AIR system; PDF copies are used for employee furnishing, review, and archive packets.
